## Brand & Style
This design system marries the disciplined precision, restraint, and ergonomic polish of Apple's Human Interface Guidelines with an ethereal, antigravity cyber-wellness aesthetic. It is tailored for high-performance bio-optimization, neural analytics, and futuristic longevity monitoring. 

The emotional tone balances clinical certainty with weightless tranquility. The interface feels deep, cosmic, and intensely private—less like a traditional medical record and more like an elevated bio-computational cockpit. 

### Visual Expression
- **Weightless Glass Surfaces:** Layered, hyper-refined translucent vessels floating over deep midnight space, simulating optical lens depth rather than heavy physical drops.
- **Specular Highlights & Inner Glows:** Subtly illuminated top borders and soft interior radial gradients that mimic light catching edge-curved crystal glass.
- **Micro-Precision Instrumentation:** Tight, scannable data layouts, pill-encapsulated telemetry, and hyper-legible metric displays inspired by aerospace and performance computing.
- **Atmospheric Chromatic Fields:** Non-intrusive, deep-space background atmospheric blurs (electric indigo, cyan, and violet) that bring warmth and life to an otherwise pitch-black palette.

## Colors
The palette operates exclusively in dark mode, built upon deep cosmic midnights that allow vibrant luminescence and bio-data tokens to radiate without causing optical fatigue.

### Color Architecture
- **Canvas & Spatial Foundation:** 
  - `Base 0`: `#06080F` (True space abyss, deepest background)
  - `Base 1`: `#0A0D18` (Surface tier 1, secondary background fill)
  - `Base 2`: `#12172A` (Surface tier 2, elevated container backplate)
- **Primary Accent (Cyan Flux - `#00F5FF`):** Represents optimal bio-equilibrium, telemetry data points, positive progress, and focused action targets. Emits a subtle outer glow (`0 0 16px rgba(0, 245, 255, 0.35)`) in active states.
- **Secondary Accent (Electric Violet - `#7928CA`):** Signals deep neural states, bio-frequency measurements, high-tier membership status, and structural gradients. Blends into `#5900B3` on darker edges.
- **Tertiary Accent (Soft Rose Crisis - `#FF3366`):** Critical alerts, threshold warnings, elevated cardiac or bio-stress events, and urgent interventions. Never used for decorative accents; preserved strictly for visual triage.
- **Neutrals & Translucents:**
  - `Glass Stroke Low`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` (Default hairline border)
  - `Glass Stroke High`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.20)` (Hover/Active edge highlight)
  - `Glass Fill Base`: `rgba(10, 13, 24, 0.65)` (Standard card backplate)
  - `Text Primary`: `#F8FAFC` (Pure legibility, high contrast)
  - `Text Secondary`: `#94A3B8` (Labels, metadata, dimensional tags)
  - `Text Muted`: `#475569` (Inactive placeholders, grid track indicators)

## Typography
Typography channels the geometric neutrality and structural clarity of modern Apple typography through **Manrope**, paired with the calibrated technical telemetry of **JetBrains Mono**.

### Typographic Rules
- **Hierarchical Balance:** Headlines use negative tracking and proportional line-heights to lock titles tightly, evoking an authoritative editorial voice.
- **Biometric Readouts:** For large numeric displays (heart rate variability, cellular recovery percentages, vo2 max), use `data-metric-lg` and `data-metric-md` styled with tabular numerals (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent horizontal layout shift during live updates.
- **Telemetry Labels:** Sub-labels, sensor states, status pills, and axis markings use uppercase `label-mono` or `label-mono-xs` to introduce a distinct computational contrast against the organic curves of Manrope.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a flexible, 12-column adaptive grid designed to prioritize spatial openness, fluid dashboard metrics, and uncrowded interaction targets.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Layouts expand to structured modular cards, allowing side-by-side metric tiles and bio-analytic stream graphs.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns with `2rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Complex charts collapse into stacked or tabbed orientations.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` outer canvas padding and `0.75rem` gutters. Cards shift to full-width horizontal tracking cards with vertically stacked parameters.

### Rhythm & Vertical Stacking
- Component internals rely on strict multiples of 4px. Element padding maps directly to:
  - Tight pill badges / controls: `space-xs` (4px) vertical, `space-sm` (8px) horizontal.
  - Interactive fields & list items: `space-sm` (8px) vertical, `space-md` (16px) horizontal.
  - Metric containers & telemetry cards: `space-lg` (24px) uniform interior inset.
  - Canvas section offsets: `space-xl` (40px) vertical spacing between independent metric widgets.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is achieved through optical refractive layers, fluid luminescence, and atmospheric background depths rather than heavy opaque drop shadows.

### The Antigravity Glass Formula
1. **Base Layer (L0):** Solid `#06080F` canvas overlaid with fixed, oversized radial gradients:
   - Primary glow: `radial-gradient(circle at 15% 20%, rgba(0, 245, 255, 0.07) 0%, transparent 60%)`
   - Secondary glow: `radial-gradient(circle at 85% 75%, rgba(121, 40, 202, 0.09) 0%, transparent 65%)`
2. **Elevated Glass Container (L1):** 
   - Background fill: `rgba(10, 13, 24, 0.65)`
   - Backdrop filter: `blur(20px) saturate(180%)`
   - Perimeter stroke: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
   - Specular top highlight: `inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)`
   - Ambient drop: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`
3. **Floating Overlays & Modals (L2):** 
   - Background fill: `rgba(18, 23, 42, 0.85)`
   - Backdrop filter: `blur(32px) saturate(200%)`
   - Perimeter stroke: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`
   - Inner glow: `inset 0 0 12px 0 rgba(0, 245, 255, 0.06)`
   - Specular reflection: `inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.28)`
   - Shadow: `0 30px 60px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85), 0 0 24px 0 rgba(0, 245, 255, 0.08)`

## Shapes
Shapes express physical smoothness and high-ergonomic tactile curves. The global roundedness setting is pill-oriented (`3`), echoing Apple hardware curvature and organic anatomical contours.

### Shape Hierarchy
- **Pills (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** All badges, interactive chips, primary buttons, status indicators, and slider thumbs.
- **Large Modular Containers (`rounded-xl` / 48px):** Primary viewport cards, biometric graph stages, modal dialogs, and persistent bottom utility docks.
- **Medium Inputs & Segmented Controls (`rounded-lg` / 24px - 32px):** Form text inputs, search modules, and segmented view switchers.
- **Inner Sub-elements (`rounded-md` / 16px):** Nested mini-metrics within larger dashboard cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Cyber:** Fully rounded pill (`9999px`), background: linear-gradient(`135deg`, `#00F5FF 0%`, `#00A3FF 100%`), text: `#06080F` (bold weight). Top specular line `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.4)`. Outer cyan glow on hover (`0 0 20px rgba(0, 245, 255, 0.4)`), translateY(-1px).
- **Secondary Glass:** Fully rounded pill, background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`, border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text: `#F8FAFC`. Hover: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, border: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`.
- **Destructive / Crisis:** Deep crimson glass tint `rgba(255, 51, 102, 0.15)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 51, 102, 0.4)`, text `#FF3366`. Outer pulse on emergency states.

### Biometric Badges & Chips
- Fully rounded pills with `label-mono-xs` text. Height strictly 24px or 28px.
- **Nominal/Active:** Border `1px solid rgba(0, 245, 255, 0.3)`, background `rgba(0, 245, 255, 0.08)`, text `#00F5FF`. Accompanied by a 6px pulsing dot.
- **Neural/Rest:** Border `1px solid rgba(121, 40, 202, 0.3)`, background `rgba(121, 40, 202, 0.12)`, text `#C084FC`.
- **Critical/Warning:** Border `1px solid rgba(255, 51, 102, 0.4)`, background `rgba(255, 51, 102, 0.12)`, text `#FF3366`.

### Cards & Telemetry Panels
- Multi-layered frosted glass (`rgba(10, 13, 24, 0.65)`, `backdrop-blur: 20px`), curvature: `32px` (`rounded-xl`).
- Perimeter border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Header section contains an uppercase `label-mono` category and an action icon or status pill.
- Main interior features `data-metric-lg` paired with a miniature vector trend sparkline rendered in `#00F5FF` with an area gradient fading to `transparent`.

### Inputs & Search Bars
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) containers with `backdrop-blur: 16px`.
- Background: `rgba(18, 23, 42, 0.6)`. Hairline border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Typographic style: `body-md` in `#F8FAFC`, placeholder in `#475569`.
- Focused state: Border transition to `#00F5FF`, with an ambient inner shadow `inset 0 0 8px rgba(0, 245, 255, 0.25)`.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Selectors
- **Toggles / Switches:** Capsule frame with track in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Thumb is a solid pure white circle with a soft directional glow. Active track transitions smoothly into `#00F5FF`.
- **Segmented Control:** Enclosed pill housing with an animated sliding thumb pill in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, creating an effortless Apple-style dynamic fluid toggle.

### Health Waveform & Radial Gauges
- Circular vitals gauges use `stroke-linecap: round`, background track at `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`, and primary arc driven by an angular gradient cycling from `#00F5FF` to `#7928CA`.
- Real-time ECG or neural frequency monitors render on a razor-thin 1px grid backdrop (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` grid cells) with glowing pulse heads.
